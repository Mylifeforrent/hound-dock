Suggested correction (not applied): append an explicit "Closing condition" and "Gate" to both canonical entries and mirror them in §12.2, then update the self-check. **File:line:** `docs/00_setup/open_items.md:307–323`; `docs/01_product/business_model.md:872–886`, `:964`.

The confirmed chat capability is therefore under-modeled, and the CAP table does not cover an adjacent capability that the Draft PRD and prototype both show. Suggested correction (not applied): add an in/out CAP row for invocation and tool-call visibility, or explicitly fold it into CAP-001 and give it a BR/flow; then update traceability. **File:line:** `docs/01_product/business_model.md:80`, `:144`, `:621`, `:926`; `docs/01_product/prd.md:45`, `:90`, `:106–110`; `docs/03_design/prototype/index.html:196–203`.
